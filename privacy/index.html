Privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Hundred Masks is an iPhone game by Aralem, developed by Mirzat Ghayrat. This policy covers the game and this website.

Playing the game

The game does not require an account. We do not operate a game server or include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not collect your moves, saved battles, tour choices, collection, or device location.

Progress on your device

The game stores progress and preferences locally, including story results, saved actions and undo history, tour progress, collection memories, and sound or accessibility preferences. This information is used to resume play and remember your choices. It is not sent to us. Cloud synchronization is not included. Removing the app removes its local data; your device’s own backup settings may affect backups.

Purchases

The game is free and has no in-app purchases. Downloading it from the App Store is handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; we do not receive your Apple Account or payment information.

Contacting support

If you email support@aralem.dev, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and troubleshoot. Support email is handled through Gmail and is retained only as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. You may ask us to delete your support correspondence. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or verification codes.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, cookies or contact forms. The fonts and images on this site are served from the site itself; nothing is loaded from third parties. GitHub may collect technical information, including visitor IP addresses, for security and service operation. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Following an external link subjects your visit to that provider’s practices.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from players, including children, and offers nothing to buy. A parent or guardian may contact us about any personal information included in a support email.

Changes and questions

If the game’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@aralem.dev.
