A little help along the road.

Questions about Hundred Masks, your purchase or a move that didn’t go as planned? Write to support@aralem.dev. Most answers are below.

Playing

Understand a move or a new mask

Tap a friendly piece to see its legal destinations. Tap the Threats control to inspect enemy attacks. Matching pieces can merge into a new mask; a Pawn reaching the far edge chooses a Knight, Bishop, Rook or Queen.

Try the free promotion lesson in The Duels or Settings. The battle’s rules button explains moving, merging, summoning and lantern scoring. If you are stuck, Hint explains the level’s goal first and, if you ask, shows a checked move.

Purchases

What is free?

The first three story chapters of the Journey are free, with guidance inside the game. One optional purchase unlocks the full game — every chapter, tour and duel. There are no subscriptions, advertising, energy timers or paid battle currency.

Purchases

Restore the full game

Open Settings in the game and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase and connect to the internet. A purchase waiting for family approval unlocks after Apple confirms it.

Restoring a purchase restores access to the full game. It does not restore a deleted local game save.

Purchases

The store is unavailable

If the game says the store is unavailable right now, your free chapters are still there. Check your connection and try again later; the purchase screen is also available from Settings → Unlock the Full Game.

Your data

Continue a saved battle or tour

The game saves after each action. Choose Resume Journey, Resume Lantern Duel or open The Lantern Tour to continue. Keep the app installed to preserve your save.

Progress stays on this device. Cloud saves and transfers between devices are not included. Deleting the app removes its local progress.

Your data

Play without an internet connection

Story battles, the daily challenge, tours and puzzles run on your device. Internet access is needed to buy or restore the full game. If the store is unavailable, the free chapters and promotion lesson remain playable.

Settings

Sound, vibration or motion

Open the game’s Settings to adjust sound effects, music, haptics and battle tips. For less motion, turn on Reduce Motion in your iPhone’s Settings → Accessibility → Motion; the game follows that setting.

Purchases

Refunds

Apple handles payments and refund decisions. For an Apple purchase refund, visit Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Contact

Report a problem

Email us with your iPhone model, iOS version, game version and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help. Never send a password, payment-card number or Apple Account verification code.
