Fact sheet

Title
Hundred Masks: Merge Chess
Developer
Aralem (independent, solo)
Platform
iPhone, iOS 18 or later
Price
Free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, plays offline
Release
Coming soon to the App Store
Genre
Chess puzzle · board · strategy
Content
18 hand-built puzzles across six chapters, a five-battle Lantern Tour and a Lantern Duel
Hook
Chess pieces merge: two Pawns become a Knight, two Knights a Bishop, up to the Queen. Masks earned from captures and merges summon allies onto the board. The rival never rolls dice, and every level has a proven perfect route.

Short description

What if two knights became a bishop? Hundred Masks: Merge Chess is a free iPhone puzzle game where chess pieces merge into stronger ones. Summon allies, outthink a rival that never rolls dice and find the perfect route through 18 hand-built puzzles across six paper worlds. No ads, no in-app purchases, plays offline.
